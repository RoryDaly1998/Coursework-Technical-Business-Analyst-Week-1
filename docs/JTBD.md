# Jobs to Be Done

## Customer control and self-service

### Selected Quote

> "Customers would pay more readily if they understood exactly what they owe and could see options."

**Source:** SN-065, Dr Lynda Smith, Operations Analyst

### JTBD Statement

When I need to address an overdue balance, I want to understand clearly what I owe and what choices are available, so that I can decide how to manage it.

**Stakeholder group:** Customer

## Case complexity, triage, and human judgment

### Selected Quote

> "We do not have a way to identify which cases are straightforward versus which require specialist handling."

**Source:** SN-039, Simon Burns, Compliance Liaison

### JTBD Statement

When a collections case needs attention, I want to identify whether it is straightforward or requires specialist handling, so that it receives the appropriate level of support.

**Stakeholder group:** Representative; Operations Manager

## Work prioritisation and contact strategy

### Selected Quote

> "We reach out to customers in a random order rather than any strategic sequence."

**Source:** SN-077, Clare Willis, Service Design Lead

### JTBD Statement

When multiple customer accounts need contact, I want to sequence outreach strategically, so that customers are contacted at an appropriate time and recovery effort is focused.

**Stakeholder group:** Representative; Operations Manager

## Follow-up tracking and case continuity

### Selected Quote

> "We have cases sitting in 'awaiting callback' status for months because the promised date was never recorded."

**Source:** SN-007, Lawrence Bennett, Finance Analyst

### JTBD Statement

When a case is awaiting a promised callback, I want to record and track the promised date, so that the callback is completed on time and the case does not remain stalled.

**Stakeholder group:** Representative; Operations Manager

## Fragmented records, inconsistent data, and audit trail

### Selected Quote

> "The audit trail is scattered across email, spreadsheets, and the legacy database, making compliance reviews a nightmare."

**Source:** SN-008, Andrea Hunt, Operations Manager

### JTBD Statement

When a compliance review requires reconstructing what happened to a case, I want to rely on a complete record of its activity across systems, so that I can verify the case history without searching fragmented records.

**Stakeholder group:** Representative; Operations Manager, Compliance

## Legacy systems, scale, and front-line productivity

### Selected Quote

> "The database cannot handle the complexity of modern lending products and customer situations."

**Source:** SN-035, Systems Administrator

### JTBD Statement

When a case involves a modern lending product or complex customer circumstances, I want to assess its details with the information and system capacity available, so that I can determine and carry out the appropriate next action.

**Stakeholder group:** Representative; Operations Manager, Finance Manager
