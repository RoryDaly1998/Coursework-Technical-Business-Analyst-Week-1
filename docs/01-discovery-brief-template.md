# Discovery Brief Template

Use this file to create your one-page discovery brief for Legacy-Trust Bank.

## 1. Problem summary

Write 80-120 words answering:
- What is happening now?
    Right now collections relies on multiple systems and several department specific workarounds. These include spreadsheets, email trails and an outdated database.
- Why is it failing at scale?
    There are too many moving parts and interacting systems. The current system relies on manual handling leading to missed follow ups, duplicated work and inconsistent data recording.
- Why does it matter financially and operationally?
    There is duplication of work leading to inflated staff costs. The current system is impossible to scale as there is a tangle of connected systems. Customer experience suffers due to slow and repetitive processes to record and provide information.

## 2. Stakeholder overview

Complete a short table like the one below.

| Stakeholder group | What they care about | How success is measured | Main worry | Evidence they will trust |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Operations leadership. | Saving reps time. | Workload for reps is reduced. | Automation which complicates the team's work further or adds no benefit. | Clear plan of how automation will save the team time and a process the team can clearly follow. |
| Team leaders and representatives. | That the project makes teammembers work easier. | Feedback of team members. | The project works great on paper but reps have to step in when problems occur. | Clearly show where rep work begins and ends with the new system. |
| Finance. | Create measurable savings. | Real cost savings realised. | Project doesn't survive real business situations and doesn't save the business money. | Evidence based report of how the project will lead to operational changes and cost savings. |
| Product and delivery. | Project is well thought through and well documented. | Creating a workable prototype. | Poor documentation or unrealistic design. | Fully traceable analysis where statements are always linked to opportunities and buildable requirements. |
| Customers. | Fast, accurate and easy to use service. | Customer feedback and retention. | New system reduces quality of support or security of data. | Using the portal improves their experience. |

## 3. Discovery questions

Write 5-7 questions that point toward evidence.

Starter examples:
- Which steps in debt recovery are high-volume and rules-driven enough for self-service?
- Where do spreadsheets and manual handoffs create duplicate work?
- Which baseline metrics best show operational waste and revenue leakage?

- What are some of the complexities of customer cases that provide the most challenge to reps?
- What information is crucial for the customers to have access to?
- What current processes are there to maintain data compliance with regulations?
- How many potential users does the new process need to be able to scale for?
- How is the current database structured and what limitations does the 20 year old software have?
- What is the current approval structure for handling customer accounts?
- What is the current appeal process for a customer unhappy with how their account has been handled?
